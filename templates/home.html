Pose Score (kcal/mol) Sulfonamide N – Zn Zinc contact
A lower score means tighter predicted binding. Sulfonamide inhibitors bind carbonic anhydrase by coordinating the active-site zinc, so a pose without that contact is probably not the real binding mode even when it scores well. In our validation on 3HS4, the near-native pose (1.6 Å from the crystal structure) ranked second, not first — so check the poses, not just the top score.
