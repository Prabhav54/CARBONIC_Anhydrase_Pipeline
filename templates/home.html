How to read these numbers
The score out of 10 is a percentile, not a probability. It places the predicted affinity inside the range actually measured against {{ iso.label }} in ChEMBL. 7.0 means the molecule is predicted stronger than 70% of known {{ iso.label }} inhibitors; 3.0 means weaker than 70% of them. It says nothing about whether the molecule is safe, selective or synthesisable.
0 weakest5 typical known inhibitor10 strongest
pAff is the predicted affinity as -log10(molar), pooling IC50 and Ki the way ChEMBL does; nM is the same number as a concentration. Lower nM is more potent.
Lipinski's Rule of 5 asks four questions about oral drug-likeness: molecular weight ≤ 500, LogP ≤ 5, hydrogen-bond donors ≤ 5, acceptors ≤ 10. Lipinski's own formulation tolerates one violation, which is why a molecule breaking a single rule is still marked drug-like. The chips show each measured value, and a failing one is highlighted.
QED is a 0 to 1 summary of drug-likeness; around 0.5 and above is typical of marketed oral drugs.

Model coverage: which isoforms can be scored, and why some cannot
Potency is predicted by a Random Forest and LSTM ensemble over the molecule's fingerprint plus four descriptors of the target protein, so the same molecule scores differently against different isoforms. An isoform is only scored if the model demonstrably predicts it: it needs enough measured molecules and a usable R² on held-out molecules of that isoform. Where it does not, the structure can still be docked and its site inspected, and the potency number is withheld rather than guessed.

What the docking does, and what it cannot tell you
Docking uses AutoDock Vina with the AutoDock4Zn force field. The receptor is protonated and its zinc maps built for your structure on demand, which is why the first molecule takes about a minute.
Read the zinc distance, not just the score. Carbonic anhydrase inhibitors work by coordinating the catalytic zinc with a sulfonamide nitrogen at roughly 2 Å. A pose that scores well but sits 5 Å away is probably not the real binding mode, so every pose is shown with its zinc distance and the residues it touches.
Limits: the receptor is rigid, solvent is implicit, and scores are a ranking aid rather than binding free energies. Potency is interpolation from measured data, not discovery.
